Privacy Policy

How Vectora handles analytics, attribution, and basic site data.

Last updated: 2026-05-09. Vectora is a public information and verification site. We do not ask visitors for wallet seed phrases, private keys, or remote wallet access.

Information We Collect

We may receive standard browser and device information, page URLs, referral sources, and campaign parameters such as utm_source, utm_campaign, and utm_term. If you contact us through public channels, we receive whatever information you choose to send.

Analytics And Advertising Attribution

The site uses Google Analytics to understand page usage and link clicks. When traffic arrives from Coinzilla campaigns, Coinzilla attribution may be used to measure ad performance and conversion events such as opening the official Raydium route.

Cookies And Local Storage

Analytics and advertising tools may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser storage to preserve attribution and measure whether campaigns are working. You can limit or clear these through your browser settings.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect seed phrases or private keys.
  • We do not provide wallet recovery or wallet validation flows.
  • We do not ask for private-sale deposits through DMs.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the official public channels listed on the Contact page. For verification, start from Proof Pack or Token Info.
