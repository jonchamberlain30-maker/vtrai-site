Seed example Source gap

Audience size is not due diligence.

Seed example Control gap

Safe language is not proof. Control evidence is.

Claims about safety, liquidity, or authority should resolve to public receipts. A clean phrase is weaker than a checkable control state.

Seed example Evidence gap

A violent move can be the warning, not the proof.

Momentum can hide the actual question: what changed, where is it visible, and can someone verify it from public sources?

What Counts

Useful submissions make a specific proof problem easier for another person to verify.

Source Gap

The official account, site, docs, or announcement does not clearly resolve to the same token address being promoted.

Route Gap

The swap route, pool, or tracker page points at a token that does not match the stated official mint or contract.

Control Gap

Mint authority, freeze authority, liquidity lock, or ownership claims are being repeated without public receipts.
