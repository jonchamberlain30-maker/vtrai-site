Token contract checker

Before buying a token, make the contract prove it belongs there.

A ticker can be copied. A logo can be copied. A reply can be convincing. The contract or mint is the thing to verify against the official source, explorer, DEX route, liquidity, permissions, holders, and control risk.

Open Token Check 5-Minute Checklist

The contract check

  1. Find the contract or mint from an official source.
  2. Compare it with the explorer page.
  3. Compare it with the DEX route or pair page.
  4. Check liquidity, holders, fees, and recent pool behavior.
  5. Read upgrade, pause, mint, freeze, blacklist, or owner controls where available.
  6. Check wallet approvals before connecting to a site or app.

Common mistakes

  • Copying a pair address when you meant to copy the token contract.
  • Trusting a chart before checking the official source.
  • Assuming a verified contract means low risk.
  • Approving a wallet action because the page looked familiar.

The contract check is not glamorous. Neither is finding out the route was wrong after the transaction.
