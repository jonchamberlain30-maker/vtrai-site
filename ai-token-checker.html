The AI Token Check

  1. Find the token from a project-owned website, docs page, or verified account.
  2. Copy the exact contract or mint. Ignore screenshots and reply-thread addresses.
  3. Compare the contract on the explorer and swap route.
  4. Check liquidity depth and holder concentration before trusting the chart.
  5. Read what permissions the app or agent wants before connecting a wallet.
  6. Look for public proof of the AI claim: product, code, logs, docs, or repeatable demos.

AI Token Red Flags

  • The agent claim is huge but the contract source is hard to find.
  • The app asks for wallet permissions before explaining what it can do.
  • The project markets autonomy but does not show who can upgrade or pause contracts.
  • The chart is the main proof. Very cinematic. Usually expensive.
If the product is "AI" but the proof is screenshots, slow down.

AI Token Checker FAQ

How do I check if an AI token is legit?

Start from the official source, copy the exact contract, compare it on the explorer and route, then check liquidity, permissions, wallet approvals, and the public proof behind the AI claim.

What is the biggest red flag in AI crypto projects?

A loud AI story with a contract, route, or permission path that is hard to verify.

Should I connect my wallet to test an AI agent token?

Not until the contract, source, route, and approval request make sense. Verification comes before wallet interaction.

Are AI tokens on Base safer than other chains?

Not automatically. Base can offer cleaner UX, but the same contract, route, liquidity, and approval checks still apply.
