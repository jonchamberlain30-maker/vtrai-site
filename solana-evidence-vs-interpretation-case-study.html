Why the distinction matters

A transaction contains signatures, account addresses and compiled instructions. A token mint exposes defined authority roles. But economic labels and market claims often require additional mappings, sources or observations. A Solana forum researcher demonstrated this with routed execution: the same landed records produced different coverage when the venue registry changed.

The practical rule is simple: say what the source established, name the interpretation layer, timestamp the observation, and leave the rest unknown. A saved receipt preserves the earlier assessment; it does not become a current assessment later.
