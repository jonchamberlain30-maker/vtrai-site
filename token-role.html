Narrative System

What to say repeatedly without sounding promotional.

The best version is short enough for posts, ads, articles, and menus. Same spine everywhere, different surface each time.

  • Public proof first. Use source, mint, route, authority, and liquidity context before trusting the chart.
  • Save before deciding. A token that matters should survive a second check after the noise changes.
  • Proof Hunt turns confusion into examples. Publish unclear trails that help the next person verify faster.
  • Official route only. Make the verified $VTRAI path easier to find than any screenshot or copied address.
  • No airdrop bait. Recognition and useful public examples are safer than promising rewards for posts.
  • No performance claims. The product should earn attention by being useful, not by implying investment outcomes.
