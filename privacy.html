Privacy Policy

How Vectora handles analytics, attribution, and basic site data.

Last updated: 2026-09-05. Vectora is a public information and verification site. We do not ask visitors for wallet seed phrases, private keys, or remote wallet access.

Information We Collect

We may receive standard browser and device information, page URLs, referral sources, and campaign parameters such as utm_source, utm_campaign, and utm_term. If you contact us through public channels, we receive whatever information you choose to send.

Analytics And Advertising Attribution

The site uses Google Analytics to understand page usage and link clicks. When traffic arrives from advertising campaigns, campaign attribution may be used to measure performance and conversion events such as opening the official Raydium route.

Cookies And Local Storage

Analytics and advertising tools may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser storage to preserve attribution and measure whether campaigns are working. You can limit or clear these through your browser settings.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect seed phrases or private keys.
  • We do not provide wallet recovery or wallet validation flows.
  • We do not ask for private-sale deposits through DMs.

Voluntary Research Pilot

The pilot form collects your chosen research role, self-reported task outcome, written feedback, next step, coarse device category and campaign source. It does not ask for contact details or wallet ownership. Vectora and its AI assistant review answers privately to improve the product; they are not testimonials. Do not submit sensitive or identifying information.

A random submission reference is kept in your browser so a retry does not duplicate your answer and you can withdraw it using the pilot page. The application stores no IP address with the response. Hosting providers may process connection data as part of normal service operation. The pilot page has no analytics tags; the main checker uses the analytics described above.

Research answers are deleted after 30 days by a six-hour cleanup job, within 31 days under normal operation. They are also removed on withdrawal and before research review if older than 30 days. Clearing browser storage removes the local withdrawal reference; use the official contact channel for help. Pilot answers are not added to a marketing list.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the official public channels listed on the Contact page. For verification, start from Proof Pack or Token Info.
